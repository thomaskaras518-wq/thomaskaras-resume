GOVERNANCE · SEMANTIC · AUTONOMY

2025—

The current station, and the tallest block on the map. Everything earlier was practice for this: one definition of every number, and a platform where asking a question no longer requires an analyst.

Observations

  1. Co-led the end-to-end redesign and reconstruction of the enterprise data warehouse layers in dbt — designing staging, base and data mart models from the ground up to eliminate data gaps and cut reporting cost.
  2. Migrated all analytical workflows and AI data consumption from a Postgres OLTP system to Snowflake's OLAP platform, improving access performance and scalability.
  3. Established a semantic layer so a number means the same thing to a dashboard, an analyst and an agent — the precondition for trusting any of the three.
  4. Implemented a comprehensive data catalog across the new layers to centralise metadata, improve discoverability, and hold governance across the organisation.
  5. Deployed Hex as a self-serve platform for AI-powered report generation and conversational analytics, so business users explore data without queuing behind an analyst.
  6. Integrated autonomous AI agents to execute complex analytical tasks, reducing turnaround from weeks to under an hour.
  7. Directed the centralised reporting analyst team, standardising workflows and governance practice to maintain a single source of truth across enterprise analytics.
